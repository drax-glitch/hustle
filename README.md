# HUSTLE

### Turn Real Life Into Your Game.

HUSTLE is an RPG-powered productivity and personal growth platform that turns real-life goals and daily actions into quests, XP, character progression, boss battles, and an evolving world.

Instead of simply checking off a to-do list, HUSTLE makes progress feel like an adventure.

---

## 🎮 The Idea

Traditional productivity apps tell you what you need to do.

HUSTLE asks:

> **What if your real life was the game?**

Your everyday actions become quests.

Complete quests to earn:

- ⚡ XP
- 🪙 Gold
- 📈 Attributes
- 🧠 Skills
- 🏆 Achievements
- 🔥 Streaks

Your progress changes your character, your world, and the challenges you're fighting.
